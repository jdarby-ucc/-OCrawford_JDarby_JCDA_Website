Current programmes

Practical support in four priority areas.

Project descriptions and results show how JCDA shares its work with residents, volunteers and community partners.

Education

Ready for School

Provides learning supplies, homework support and parent information sessions before the new school term.

185 students assisted
Youth

Play, Lead, Grow

Uses football and mentorship to teach teamwork, responsibility and healthy decision-making.

96 youth participants
Environment

Clean Coast, Clean Community

Organizes clean-up activities and teaches volunteers how everyday waste affects drains, gullies and the sea.

3.4 tonnes of waste removed
Outreach

Neighbour to Neighbour

Coordinates food support, information referrals and wellness checks for vulnerable residents.

240 family packages delivered
