About the Jamaica Community Development Association

A community-focused non-profit organization built around participation, practical service and respect for Jamaican communities.

Our story

Community development with local voices at the centre.

JCDA was established in 2018 by a small network of educators, youth mentors and community volunteers in Kingston. The group wanted a practical way to coordinate outreach activities and give residents a stronger role in local development.

What began as a back-to-school drive grew into year-round programmes supporting youth, families, environmental projects and community spaces. JCDA now works with residents and local partners to plan activities that respond to identified needs.

JCDA works with residents, volunteers and local partners to develop programmes that respond to practical community needs.

Our purpose

Guided by service, inclusion and accountability.

M

Mission

To strengthen Jamaican communities through education, youth development, environmental action and outreach programmes shaped by local participation.

V

Vision

Connected and resilient communities where residents have the knowledge, opportunity and support needed to thrive.

C

Core commitment

To listen before acting, use resources responsibly and treat every community member with dignity and respect.

Our values

The standards behind every programme.

  • Respect: Every voice deserves to be heard.
  • Service: Community needs come before recognition.
  • Integrity: Decisions and resources must be accountable.
  • Inclusion: Programmes should be accessible and welcoming.
  • Partnership: Lasting progress requires cooperation.
  • Resilience: Communities can adapt and rebuild together.
Our journey

JCDA development timeline

Key milestones showing how the organization expanded.

  1. 2018

    JCDA established

    Volunteers organized the first Kingston back-to-school drive.

  2. 2020

    Family outreach expanded

    Food and care packages were coordinated during a period of need.

  3. 2023

    Youth skills programme launched

    Weekend workshops introduced digital, leadership and career skills.

  4. 2026

    Community partnership network

    JCDA began coordinating projects with more local groups across Kingston.
