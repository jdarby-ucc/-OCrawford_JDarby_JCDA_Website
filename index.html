Welcome to JCDA

Local people creating practical change.

We believe lasting development begins when community members are included in the decisions that affect them.

Our programmes are designed around real Jamaican needs: helping students prepare for school, creating safe spaces for young people, organizing neighbourhood clean-ups and supporting families during difficult periods.

Every activity is guided by respect, accountability and the belief that strong communities are built together.

Our focus

Development that reaches everyday life.

JCDA works across four connected areas that help residents build skills, confidence and safer communities.

01

Education support

Homework clubs, back-to-school assistance and practical workshops for students and parents.

02

Youth development

Sports, mentorship and leadership activities that give young people positive spaces to grow.

03

Environmental care

Community clean-ups, tree planting and public education about responsible waste disposal.

04

Family outreach

Food drives, referral support and community activities that strengthen connections between families.
